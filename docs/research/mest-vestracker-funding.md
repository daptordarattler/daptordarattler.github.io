# Vestracker — 2014 Seed Funding (MEST Ghana + Swedish co-investor)

Background notes for the Vestracker entry on the CV.

- Co-investors:
  - **MEST** (Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology), Accra, Ghana
  - A Swedish co-investor (name not disclosed in CV)
- Year: **2014** — same year Vestracker spun out of the MEST EIT cohort
- Later: a Chilean innovation grant supporting international expansion
  (Start-Up Chile or similar — already mentioned in earlier CV revisions)

User was an Entrepreneur-in-Training at MEST around 2013-2014 (per Education
section), so Vestracker fits the standard MEST flow: complete the year-long
EIT program → top teams pitch → MEST takes equity, writes a seed cheque,
and incubates the company.

## What MEST is (verified via search)

- Full name: **Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology**
- Founded: 2008, in Accra, Ghana
- Founder: Jorn Lyseggen (CEO of Meltwater Group, Norway)
- Twin model: 1) **EIT** — 12-month intensive entrepreneurship + software
  engineering programme; 2) **MEST Africa Seed Fund / Incubator** — invests
  in the strongest teams from each cohort
- Typical 2014-era ticket: ~USD $50K-$100K from MEST itself, often
  syndicated with external angels/family offices (matches the combined-$100K
  total here)
- Notable portfolio: Asoriba, meQasa, Kudobuzz, Tress, BezoMoney,
  Complete Farmer (publicly known; Vestracker is among many smaller
  alumni without strong online footprint)
- Headquarters: Accra, Ghana — operations across Lagos, Nairobi, Cape Town
